ReactionLab — User Guide

Welcome to ReactionLab. This guide walks you through everything you need to know to get the most out of the platform — from creating your account to completing topics and earning badges.

Getting Started

Creating an Account

To access ReactionLab's interactive content, you need a free account. To sign up:

  • Click Sign Up in the top navigation bar.
  • Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a password.
  • Click Create Account to complete registration.

Once registered, you will be taken directly to the Topics page where you can start learning.

Logging In

If you already have an account:

  • Click Log In in the top navigation bar.
  • Enter your email and password.
  • Click Log In to access your account.

Your progress and badges are saved to your account and will be there every time you return.

Exploring Topics

The Topic Library

After logging in, you land on the Topics page. This is your learning hub — all available chemistry topics are listed here, each represented as a card showing the topic name, a short description, and your current progress.

Topic States

Each topic card can be in one of several states:

  • Locked — this topic is not yet available. Complete earlier topics to unlock it.
  • Available — the topic is ready to start. Click the card to begin.
  • In Progress — you have started this topic but not yet completed all its visualizations.
  • Completed — you have viewed all steps in every visualization for this topic and earned its badge.

Filtering Topics

You can filter the topic list using the tabs at the top of the library:

  • All — shows every topic regardless of state.
  • Available — shows only topics you can currently access.
  • Completed — shows topics you have already finished.

Using Visualizations

Opening a Visualization

Click on any available topic card to open its visualization page. The page is split into two panels:

  • Left panel — lists all the reactions within the topic. Click a reaction to load it.
  • Main canvas — displays the interactive step-by-step visualization of the selected reaction.

Navigating Steps

Each reaction is broken down into individual steps that walk you through the chemistry. Use the navigation controls on the main canvas to move between steps:

  • Click Next to advance to the following step.
  • Click Back to return to the previous step.
  • The step counter shows your current position (e.g. Step 2 of 5).

Each step shows atoms and molecules involved in the reaction, colour-coded by element to help you identify them at a glance.

Completing a Reaction

A reaction is marked as complete once you have viewed every step from beginning to end. You must go through all steps in order — skipping ahead is not counted as completion. Once all reactions within a topic are completed, the topic itself is marked as done and you earn its badge.

Tracking Your Progress

Progress Indicators

Each topic card displays a circular progress indicator showing how many of its reactions you have completed. For example, if a topic has 4 reactions and you have finished 2, the indicator shows 50%.

Achievement Badges

Completing a topic earns you an achievement badge. Badges are displayed on your profile and on the topic card itself once unlocked. They are a record of everything you have learned on the platform.

Badges cannot be lost — once a topic is completed and a badge is earned, it stays on your profile permanently.

Your Account

Profile and Settings

Your account page shows your profile information, earned badges, and overall progress across all topics. You can access it by clicking your name or avatar in the navigation bar.

Updating Your Information

To update your name, email, or password, go to your account settings. Changes take effect immediately.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account from the account settings page. Deleting your account is permanent — all progress and badges will be removed and cannot be recovered.

Getting Help

If you run into an issue not covered in this guide, there are a few ways to get support:

  • Check the FAQ for answers to the most common questions.
  • Reach out via the Contacts page to send us a message directly.

We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
